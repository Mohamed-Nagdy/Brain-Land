# Brain Land — what marketing may say

The single evidence map is `../store-assets/metadata/claims-evidence.md` (claim → code/test), mirrored in `Company/strategy/inventory/claims.json` (CLM-brain-land-*). Use only those claims, in those words or narrower.

Voice: warm, plain, parent-to-parent. Arabic first (Modern Standard Arabic that reads naturally in Egypt and the Gulf), English second.

Always:
- Speak to parents and carers, never to children.
- Say "free, with child-directed ads between missions" wherever ads come up; never imply the game is ad-free.
- Use Western digits in marketing copy (Company rule); in-game screenshots may show either style.

Never: learning outcomes, "smarter", "brain development", IQ, "teacher approved", "best"/"#1"/«أفضل», guarantees, «بلا إعلانات» / "no ads", download or rating numbers, fake reviews, people or children in imagery.
