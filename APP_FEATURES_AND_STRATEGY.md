## 📱 Existing Features
*Current capabilities of the application:*

### 🗺️ Core Navigation
*   **World Map:** Interactive map with different zones (Forest, Mountain, River, Desert, Shape Valley).
*   **Fancy Navigation:** Animated Floating Action Buttons (FABs) for Settings, Daily Rewards, and Progress.

### 🎮 Gameplay Zones
*   **Forest Zone:** Intro levels.
*   **Mountain Zone:** Higher difficulty.
*   **River Zone:** Flow-based puzzles.
*   **Desert Zone:** Time-based challenges.
*   **Shape Valley:** Geometry puzzles.

### 👤 Player Progression
*   **Stats:** Total Stars, Total Coins, Levels Completed.
*   **Streak System:** 7-day login streak with increasing rewards.
*   **Badges & Achievements:** Visual rewards for milestones.
*   **Zone Progress:** Track completion % per zone.

### 🎁 Rewards & Customization
*   **Daily Rewards:** 7-day cycle with coins and "Weekly Surprise Box".
*   **Unlockables:**
    *   **Pets:** Collectible companions (Dog, Cat, Bird, etc.).
    *   **Stickers:** Virtual stickers for achievements.
    *   **Avatar Items:** Hats, glasses, shirts to customize the player profile.

### ⚙️ Settings
*   **Audio Control:** Music and Sound Effects volume sliders.
*   **Mute Toggle:** Quick silence option.
*   **Debug Tools:** (Hidden) Tools to populate data and test rewards.

## 💡 Retention & Monetization Strategy

### 🆘 Help Methods (Power-ups)
*Ways users can spend coins to progress, creating a "Coin Sink" that drives ad views.*

| Power-up | Description | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **💡 Hint** | Shows the first step or a clue for the puzzle. | **50 Coins** |
| **🛑 Time Freeze** | Pauses the timer for 15 seconds (for timed levels). | **100 Coins** |
| **⏭️ Skip Level** | Instantly completes a hard level (1 star reward only). | **200 Coins** |
| **❤️ Second Chance** | Revive after failing a level without restarting. | **150 Coins** |
| **🔍 X-Ray Vision** | Reveals hidden objects or answers for 5 seconds. | **75 Coins** |

### 📺 Ad Integration Strategy (Monetization)
*How to use ads "correctly" - rewarding the user, not annoying them.*

#### 1. Rewarded Video Ads (High Value)
*User chooses to watch an ad for a specific benefit.*
*   **"Get Free Coins":** Watch an ad in the shop/map to get **+100 Coins**.
*   **"Double Reward":** Watch an ad after completing a level to **2x** the stars/coins earned.
*   **"Revive":** Watch an ad to continue a failed level instead of paying 150 coins.
*   **"Daily Bonus Doubler":** Watch an ad to double the Daily Login Reward.

#### 2. Interstitial Ads (Natural Breaks)
*Full-screen ads shown at natural transition points.*
*   **Frequency:** Show only after every 3-4 completed levels.
*   **Logic:** Do NOT show if the user fails (avoids frustration). Only show after success.

#### 3. Banner Ads (Passive)
*   **Location:** Bottom of the Menu or World Map screens.
*   **Avoid:** Do not place in gameplay screens where accidental clicks happen.

### 🔄 The Core Loop
1.  **Play:** Kid plays levels, earns small amount of coins.
2.  **Stuck:** Kid gets stuck on a hard level.
3.  **Need:** Kid wants a **Hint (50 coins)** but has only 20 coins.
4.  **Solution:** Kid sees "Watch Ad for +100 Coins" button.
5.  **Action:** Watches ad -> Gets Coins -> Buys Hint -> Solves Level.
6.  **Result:** Happy User (Progressed), Happy Developer (Ad Revenue).
